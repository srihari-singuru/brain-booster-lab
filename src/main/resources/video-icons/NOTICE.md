# Video icon sources

`like.png`, `share.png`, and `subscribe.png` are rasterized from Google Material Icons:

- https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/tree/master/src/action/thumb_up
- https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/tree/master/src/social/share
- https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/tree/master/src/av/subscriptions

The source icon set is licensed under Apache-2.0. The local copies keep video rendering offline and reproducible.
