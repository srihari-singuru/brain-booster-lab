# Video icon sources

`like.svg`, `share.svg`, and `comment.svg` are Google Material Icons sourced from:

- https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/tree/master/src/action/thumb_up
- https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/tree/master/src/social/share
- https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/tree/master/src/communication/comment

Google Material Icons are licensed under Apache-2.0. The SVG files are rasterized
locally at 512×512 when the renderer starts, keeping output crisp at video size
while rendering offline.

`youtube.png` is the full-colour YouTube play icon cropped from the transparent
padding of Google's official 2× YouTube icon sprite:

- https://developers.google.com/static/youtube/images/youtube-icons-2x.png
- https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/branding-guidelines

The YouTube mark itself is not recoloured or distorted; it is scaled with its
aspect ratio preserved. Use the icon in accordance with YouTube's brand guidelines.
